__HEADING__

Each run adds lifetime truck costs at a different weight k: a cost per meter driven and per meter of rise and fall. k = 1 is one truck's fuel for five years. Maintenance, tires, brakes and a bigger fleet all scale with these costs, so they push k up. Hover a row to pick out its route; select it to watch that search.

Drag to pan; scroll, pinch or use + and − to zoom. Hover a route for its cost.

Costs are in the tool's relative units: excavating 1 m³ of soil = 1, so at the assumed $4/m³ one unit is $4. Construction covers grading, earthwork, hairpins, bends, culverts and crossing fill. Operating is five years of truck costs at the given weight.

    Your line is costed with the same section model the search uses, at the same sample spacing: the construction of every meter follows the ground, with the cut and fill a half bench or full bench needs there. Corners are rounded to the minimum curve radius. Nothing is refused: grades over the limit, excluded ground and side slopes too steep to build are flagged instead. A drawn switchback is costed as plain road, so it usually shows as too steep; the optimizer would use a hairpin there. The optimum's figures are its full costs, with hairpins, bends, fill crossings and spoil reuse.
